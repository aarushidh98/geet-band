The Geet Indie Project Experience

Geet Indie Project, led by founder and vocalist Hrishabh Dhingra, is a premium live band known for transforming celebrations into unforgettable experiences. Every performance combines exceptional live music, audience interaction, and a carefully crafted setlist that keeps guests engaged from the first song to the last.

With over 800+ live performances across weddings, corporate events, concerts, clubs, and private celebrations, the band delivers professionalism, seamless execution, exceptional musicianship, and an atmosphere that turns every gathering into a celebration.
